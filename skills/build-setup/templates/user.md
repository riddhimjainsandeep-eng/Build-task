## How to write for them

<!-- Fill from their answers. Defaults that suit most people who do not read code: -->

1. **Give the reasoning, not only the conclusion** — what the other option was and what
   it would have cost.
2. **Plain language.** No file paths or function names in anything written for them. An
   unavoidable technical word is explained right below the paragraph it appears in.
3. **Diagrams** when explaining how something works or what happens in what order
   (Mermaid in Markdown) — if they find diagrams easier than prose.
4. **A recommendation, not a menu.** Say what you would do and why; let them disagree.
5. **"What contradicted what you believed"** is always its own heading in a report.
6. **Real questions in writing** — numbered, each with why it matters and your
   recommendation.
7. **Say "I don't know" plainly.** An invented answer they cannot check is harmful.
